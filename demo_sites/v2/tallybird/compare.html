How Tallybird compares

Tallybird vs Ledgerly

Ledgerly is the closest alternative on price. Its Starter plan is $15/month for 10 clients; Tallybird Solo is $12. Ledgerly has no automatic payment reminders.

Tallybird vs Paperplane Billing

Paperplane is built for freelancers and is strong on time tracking, which Tallybird doesn't offer. Tallybird is faster to invoice and reminds clients automatically.
